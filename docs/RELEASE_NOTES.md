# MathWizard 2.0.0: The Endless Spire

The math quiz is now an endless pixel-art roguelite.

- Climb an endless spire: every solved problem is a spell; fast answers and streaks hit harder.
- Boss every 5th floor with multi-step problems; 12 relics to choose from between floors.
- Six spell schools: Arithmetic, Fractions & percentages, Algebra, Powers & roots, Geometry, Sequences.
- Mana crystals and 7 permanent upgrades; schools unlock with crystals.
- Daily Rift (same climb for everyone each day, with streaks), Practice mode, statistics.
- Pixel art, chiptune sound effects and music, Albanian and English, relaxed mode.
- Installs over v1.0 (same signing key).
